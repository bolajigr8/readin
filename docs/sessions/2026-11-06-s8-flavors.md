## Research: how big apps take payments on Google Play (Michael: "most people will get the Play link")
- Pattern A, consumption-only (Kindle since 2022, Barnes & Noble, Netflix): no buying inside the Play app; buy on the website;
  the app may say "you can buy on our website" but no link/button. Works only for brands people already pay on the web.
- Pattern B, Google Play Billing inside the app (Spotify via Play, most subscription apps): one tap, Google account payment
  (naira Verve cards, MTN airtime billing reported; some Nigerian dollar debit cards fail on Play — FX limits). Fee: 15% on
  auto-renewing subscriptions (outside US/UK/EEA the old schedule still applies; new fees roll out later).
- Pattern C, hybrid (Spotify): Play billing in the Play app + direct payment on the web/APK; same account everywhere.
- Recommendation to Michael: Pattern C with Play Billing as the MAIN system (most users come from Play); Paystack only for the
  APK/web. Monthly auto-renewing subscription in Play; RevenueCat (free < ~$2.5k/month revenue; server code exists) to connect
  Google purchases → `premiumUntil`. Waiting for Michael's decision.
