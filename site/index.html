How to install

From Google Play (recommended)

Open the Play Store, search for ReadIn and tap Install. Updates arrive automatically. (Coming soon.)

Direct download (Android)

  1. Download the ReadIn file with the button above (most phones: the normal download).
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android may ask to allow installs from this source for your browser — allow it, then go back.
  3. If Play Protect says the app is unknown, tap More details → Install anyway.
  4. On Samsung phones, Auto Blocker may block it: Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker → off (you can turn it on again after installing).
  5. Updating later: install the new file over the old one — your books and notes stay.

Only download ReadIn from readin.micbol.org or Google Play. Our app is always signed by the same ReadIn key.

Questions

Is ReadIn free?

Yes. Reading, free classics, notes and highlights are free. ReadIn Premium adds unlimited books in your library and extra features such as the reading habit tracker.

Do my books leave my phone?

Books you import are stored on your phone. If you sync or upload a book, a copy is kept in your account so you can get it back. Read our privacy policy for the details.

Which phones are supported?

Android 7.0 or newer.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Without the app: delete your account on the web.

How do I contact you?

E-mail micboltech@gmail.com. We read every message.
